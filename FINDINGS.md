## What this does not show

`LIMITATIONS.md` has the full version. In short: 15 mechanisms on one base
repository (`cfgkit`), so the rates measured here are rates on this suite,
not an estimate of base rates on arbitrary real repositories. The paired
design supports the trap-vs-control comparison and nothing wider.
